# ⚡ PART 1: GEP 10 Grammar Master Cheat Sheet (8A to 10B)

### 📌 Unit 8A: Passives & Causatives
*   **Present Progressive Passive:** `is/are + being + V3`  
    *   *Example: The road **is being repaired**.*
*   **Causative Have:** `have + object + V3` (when someone does a service for you).  
    *   *Example: We need to **have a burglar alarm installed**.*
*   **Impersonal/Personal Passive:**  
    *   *Impersonal:* **It is said that** crime doesn't pay.  
    *   *Personal:* The burglar **is thought to be** a teenager.

### 📌 Unit 8B: Reporting Verbs (The 3 Patterns)
*   **Pattern 1: `Verb + to + Infinitive`** (agree, offer, promise, refuse, threaten).  
    *   *Example: He offered **to pay**.*
*   **Pattern 2: `Verb + Person + to + Infinitive`** (advise, persuade, remind, warn, encourage, invite).  
    *   *Example: I advised Sarah **to talk** to a lawyer.*
*   **Pattern 3: `Verb + -ing`** (admit, deny, regret, suggest, apologize for).  
    *   *Example: Margaret denied **killing** her husband.*
    *   *Example: James apologized for **being** late.*

### 📌 Unit 9A: Contrast & Purpose
*   **Although / Even though / Though** \\(\rightarrow\\) Must have `Subject + Verb`.  
    *   *Example: **Although** Penny had very little money, she bought the vase.*
*   **Despite / In spite of** \\(\rightarrow\\) Must have a `Noun / Verb-ING`.  
    *   *Example: **Despite** being only eighteen, Kelly has a degree.*
*   **Purpose:** To / In order to / So as to + `Base Verb`.  
    *   *Example: I called her **to remind** her to buy the flowers.*
*   **Negative Purpose:** **So as not to** / **In order not to** + `Base Verb` (Never write "to not"!).  
    *   *Example: Jane opened the door quietly **so as not to wake** her parents.*

### 📌 Unit 9B: Countable & Uncountable Nouns
*   **Always Uncountable** (Singular verb, NO "a/an", NO "-s"): `accommodation`, `advice`, `furniture`, `information`, `luggage`, `news`, `progress`, `traffic`, `weather`.  
    *   *Example: Adrian is looking for some cheap **accommodation**.*
    *   *Example: Let me give you a piece of **advice**.*
*   **Two-part items:** `trousers`, `scissors` take a plural verb unless preceded by **`a pair of`**.  
    *   *Example: I need to buy a new **pair of trousers**.*

### 📌 Unit 10A: Quantifiers
*   **No / None / Any:**  
    *   Use **`no`** directly before nouns (*There is **no** milk*).  
    *   Use **`none`** on its own (*None of them can swim*).  
    *   Use **`any`** for negatives (*There isn't **any** milk*).
*   **Every / All:** `Every` takes a singular noun (*Every room is clean*). `All` takes a plural noun (*All of them*).
*   **Neither (for TWO things):** Takes a **singular, positive verb**.  
    *   *Example: **Neither** of them can swim.*

### 📌 Unit 10B: Articles
*   **Institutions (Hospital, School, Prison, University):**  
    *   **No Article (-)** = Primary purpose (*I was **in hospital** for two weeks as a patient*).  
    *   **The / A** = Physical building (*I now live next door to **the school***).
*   **Geographical Rules:**  
    *   **NO Article (-)** with: Single Lakes (*Lake Constance*), Single Mountains (*Mount Everest*).  
    *   **Use "The"** with: Oceans, Rivers, Mountain Ranges, and Museums (*the British Museum*).

# 🧱 PART 2: IELTS Writing Task 1 (Process Diagram) Tips

This is your exact game plan for your brick essay or any other diagram they give you on the test!

### 🗺️ The Perfect 4-Paragraph Layout
1.  **Paragraph 1: Introduction** \\(\rightarrow\\) Rephrase the prompt (*The diagram illustrates the stages involved in...*).
2.  **Paragraph 2: Overview** \\(\rightarrow\\) Start with "**Overall...**". Summarize the main stages (*Overall, the process consists of seven main stages, beginning with digging up clay and culminating in delivery*). **Never put percentages or numbers here!**.
3.  **Paragraph 3: Detail Paragraph 1** \\(\rightarrow\\) Describe the first 60% of the diagram in chronological order.
4.  **Paragraph 4: Detail Paragraph 2** \\(\rightarrow\\) Describe the remaining stages (the final outcome, delivery, or ratios/percentages).

### 🚨 4 Golden Writing Rules
*   **Rule 1: Use the Present Simple Passive (`is/are + V3`)**  
    Since it is a general factory process, write: *Natural materials **are collected***, *The mixture **is heated***, *Bricks **are delivered***. Never write in the past tense unless a past year is written on the page.
*   **Rule 2: Match Nouns with Verbs (Subject-Verb Agreement)**  
    *   *Singular:* The process **begins**...
    *   *Plural:* The bricks **are placed**...
*   **Rule 3: Keep your transitions smooth and varied**  
    Do not repeat words. Use this sequence: *Initially* \\(\rightarrow\\) *Subsequently* \\(\rightarrow\\) *Following this* \\(\rightarrow\\) *Once [X] has occurred* \\(\rightarrow\\) *Ultimately* / *Finally*.
*   **Rule 4: Zero Speculation (Strict Factuality)**  
    Write *only* what you can see. If you see bricks going on a truck, write: *"they are loaded onto trucks for delivery"*. Do not write about "selling them to home builders" unless a home builder is drawn on the page!
